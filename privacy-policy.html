Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Drake Lousberg, also known online as Cymouz, handles information when you visit cymouz.com and its pages.

Information collected by the site

The site does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, payment information, or other personal information directly.

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and timestamps in server logs for hosting, security, and reliability.

Cookies and browser storage

The hidden easter-egg game uses browser storage to provide its features:

  • It stores game progress and settings in your browser's local storage.
  • It may request permission to show optional browser notifications for game or site events. Notifications are only used if you grant permission.
  • These items remain on your device until you delete them through your browser settings or the game's controls.

These storage items are used for site functionality and are not used by Cymouz to identify you personally. The site does not currently use a dedicated analytics cookie.

Advertising

Some pages use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure, and personalize advertisements, subject to your choices and applicable law. Google may process information about your visits to this and other websites.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Browser notifications

The hidden game may use your browser's built-in notification feature for optional game or site update notifications. Cymouz does not receive your notification permission as personal information. You can grant or revoke permission in your browser settings.

External websites

This site links to social networks, games, and other third-party websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies and may collect information independently. Cymouz is not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and Cymouz does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can disable local storage and notifications through your browser settings. You can also decline personalized advertising where the available Google controls or consent tools provide that option.

For a privacy question or request, contact Cymouz through one of the social profiles linked on the homepage. Please do not send sensitive information through a social platform.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site or its services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
